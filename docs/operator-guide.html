NomadRight Operator Guide

How to run the kiosk, the speech service and the office receiver; every switch you can change, the exact file and line it lives on, and what happens when you change it.

Jetson kioskStarted by hand in a terminal with pocketinfer-retro. Code: ~/Retro/NOMADRIGHT/pocket-infer-sw. Switches: python/pocketinfer/applications/nomad_right/constants.py. Forms and questions: nomadright/forms/build_forms.py → forms.json.
Speech servicebhashini_models.service (systemd, starts at boot). Hindi Conformer and Tamil Whisper ASR, TTS, on port 11400. Which ASR engine serves which language: ~/bhashini_models/asr/asr_config.json.
Office laptopreceiver.py from the pairing bundle. Accepts sealed forms on port 8443 (mutual TLS, kiosk certificate only), officer dashboard at http://localhost:8080/, files under received/. Paired today with 172.16.104.198.

1 · Run it

A. On the Jetson, every day

  1. Check the speech service. It starts at boot and warms the Tamil model itself.

    systemctl is-active bhashini_models.service     # expect: active
sudo systemctl restart bhashini_models.service  # only if it is not

  2. Start the kiosk in a terminal and leave that terminal open; the log stays in it.

    pocketinfer-retro

    Wait for two lines, about 50 s after start: RECEIVER paired and READY - hold button to speak. If the first says not paired, see section 3.

  3. Stop it with Ctrl-C in that terminal. Sealed forms that were not yet acknowledged stay on disk and are sent the next time the kiosk runs.

B. On the laptop, whenever forms are expected

  1. Start the receiver in PowerShell, inside the bundle folder, and keep that window open. It prints every arrival.

    cd nomadright-receiver-bundle
python receiver.py

    First line: receiving on port 8443 (mTLS, kiosk certificate required); officer dashboard: http://localhost:8080/.

  2. Open the dashboard at http://localhost:8080/ in the laptop's browser. It refreshes every 10 s and shows: last contact from the kiosk, how many forms are still waiting on the kiosk and how many failed, the last error, and one row per received form with its file name and how many fields were left for the officer.

  3. Find the files. Each form is one JSON file: received/<date>/<form>_<session>.json, inside the bundle folder. received/index.json is the running log. A form sent twice is stored once; the second copy is answered as a duplicate.

C. On the laptop, the first time only

  1. Copy the bundle from the Jetson (the Jetson is 172.16.104.200 on the office Wi-Fi):

    scp -r stark-x@172.16.104.200:nomadright-receiver-bundle .

    The folder holds the receiver script, its private key and certificate, the CA certificate and the kiosk's public key. Keep it private.

  2. Install the one dependency (Python 3.8 or newer):

    pip install pynacl

  3. Allow the port. When Windows asks, allow Python on private networks. Otherwise add an inbound rule for TCP 8443 limited to the kiosk's address 172.16.104.200. Ping stays blocked; that is fine.

  4. Keep the laptop's address fixed at 172.16.104.198 (a DHCP reservation on the router, or a static address). The kiosk holds that address and the certificate is bound to it; if it changes, re-pair (section 3).

D. A citizen's session, what the operator does

  1. The first screen is the language page and it is Home. Tamil, Hindi or English. That one tap sets the screen, the speech recogniser, the spoken answers and the form questions for the whole visit. Every Home button returns here, and it is the only place the language changes.

  2. The menu offers Speak Your Question, Scan a Document, View All Schemes and Help. Help is five numbered steps in the chosen language. Back goes to the language page.

  3. Speak Your Question: tap the microphone, ask, tap again to stop. The answer is read aloud and shown large; Listen again replays it, Ask another question starts over, Home ends the visit.

  4. Scan a Document: hold the paper form flat and close, press the camera button. The kiosk reads the title and asks "Is this the X form?" on screen and aloud; hold the blue button and say yes or no. Then every question in the form's own order: hold the button while answering, release when done. Pressing the button while the kiosk is still speaking stops the speech and starts listening at once.

  5. Repeat, Change, Skip. Repeat says the same question again. Change reopens the answer just given so it can be replaced; it never walks further back. Skip leaves an optional field at once; a required field is asked up to three times and then left for the officer under unanswered_required, never filled with a made-up value. Home cancels the whole form and wipes its answers; sealed forms already waiting for the office are not touched.

  6. Documents: for the account number and IFSC the kiosk asks for the passbook, for the Aadhaar number the Aadhaar card. Hold it steady, press the button, confirm what it read. Repeat, Skip and Home work here too.

  7. After sending the screen shows the thank-you page until Home is pressed. If the office computer is not reachable the person hears that the form is saved and will be sent automatically (section 2).

Admin panel. The tiny "Admin" text in the bottom-right corner of the language page opens the PIN prompt (PIN 9999, checked on the device; change DEFAULT_ADMIN_PIN in python/pocketinfer/boards/hdmi.py or set POCKETINFER_ADMIN_PIN before starting the kiosk). Behind it: audio, display, device status, diagnostics, logs and the terminal. Nothing else on the screens is for the operator.

Clock. The date and time in the header come from the Jetson's own clock (timezone Asia/Kolkata), never from the network. The board's real-time clock keeps time only while the coin-cell battery on the carrier's RTC header is fitted; see the power-cut test in the rebuild report.

2 · Unsent forms: the outbox

A completed form is sealed the moment the person confirms it and is written to ~/.local/state/nomadright/outbox/ as <session>.sealed plus a small .sealed.meta file with the attempt count. The plain answers exist only in memory and are dropped right there. The kiosk then tries the laptop once; on failure a background thread retries every 30 s, waiting 10 s, 20 s, 40 s … up to 5 min between attempts. Only the laptop's acknowledgement (the SHA-256 of the sealed blob) deletes the local copy.

After 24 h unsent the pair of files is moved to outbox/failed/, still sealed and still unreadable on the kiosk, and the kiosk log says so. Nothing is ever purged automatically from failed/.

What people see. The citizen hears "saved, will be sent automatically". The officer's dashboard shows, with every attempt, "N waiting on the kiosk, M failed" and the last error. On the kiosk screen itself there is no counter; the terminal prints OUTBOX sent … expired … when something moves, and the shell shows the counts at any time:

cd ~/Retro/NOMADRIGHT/pocket-infer-sw/python
venv/bin/python -c "from pocketinfer.applications.nomad_right.formfill.outbox import PairingConfig; print(PairingConfig().outbox().status())"

Re-send a failed form once the laptop is back: move it out of failed/, drop its old meta and refresh its timestamp so it does not expire again; the next 30-s cycle sends it.

cd ~/.local/state/nomadright/outbox
mv failed/<session>.sealed . && rm -f failed/<session>.sealed.meta && touch <session>.sealed
ChangeWhereNowEffect

3 · Pairing and re-pairing

Pairing runs once on the Jetson and creates everything both sides need: a private certificate authority, a certificate for each side, a NaCl key pair for each side, the kiosk's config and the laptop's bundle. Nothing about it is in the source tree.

cd ~/Retro/NOMADRIGHT/pocket-infer-sw
python3 tools/pair_receiver.py --receiver-ip 172.16.104.198 --device-id kiosk-01
--receiver-ip
The laptop's fixed address. Required. The receiver certificate is bound to it.
--port
Receiver port, default 8443. The kiosk config and the bundle's receiver.json both get it.
--device-id
Name in the kiosk certificate and in the officer's log, default kiosk-01. Use a different id per kiosk if there are several.
--days
Certificate validity, default 3650 (ten years).

Re-run pairing when the laptop's address changes, when the receiver moves to another PC, or when the bundle was lost. It overwrites ~/.config/nomadright/receiver.json and deletes and recreates ~/nomadright-receiver-bundle/; the previous bundle on the laptop stops being accepted, so copy the new one over (section 1C) and restart receiver.py. Restart the kiosk afterwards; it reads the pairing at start.

Check the link from the Jetson while the receiver is running; True means the certificate handshake and the fingerprint check passed:

cd ~/Retro/NOMADRIGHT/pocket-infer-sw/python
venv/bin/python -c "from pocketinfer.applications.nomad_right.formfill.outbox import PairingConfig; print(PairingConfig().outbox().transport.reachable())"

On the laptop, receiver.json in the bundle has two keys you may edit: dashboard_port (add it; default 8080) and store_dir (default received). Do not edit port there alone; it must match the kiosk's pairing, so change it with --port and re-pair.

4 · The review step

With the review on, the kiosk reads every answer back before sealing and the person can fix one by saying "change" and its number. With it off, the form is sealed and sent the moment the last field is answered; the person can still say "change" during the questions, but not after the last one.

ChangeWhereNowEffect
Read all answers back before sendingconstants.py · FORM_REVIEW_ENABLEDTrueFalse skips the read-back. Recommended on: it is the only moment an illiterate person hears the whole form.

4b · Questions nobody's rule can answer: the model, or an apology

A question goes through scheme intelligence, the intent rules, the scheme database and the retrieved scheme text first. What happens when all of those come up empty is one switch, QUERY_FALLBACK in constants.py. It has two positions and nothing in between:

PositionWhat the citizen getsWhat runs
"qwen" (as shipped)The vision-language model is asked, grounded in the retrieved scheme text or the named scheme's record; if it has nothing either, the apology below.llama-server starts on the first such question (about 20 s the first time, then answers in 2–4 s) and unloads after 5 minutes idle. Memory: about 2.4 GB while loaded, which on this device means some swap.
"sorry"The apology, at once, in the selected language: “I am sorry, I do not have information about that. Please ask me about a government scheme …”No model is ever started for questions. Scheme intelligence keeps its rule-based routes but not its model-backed complex-scenario route, and no raw retrieved fragment is read aloud as a last resort either. Ask Chatbot (camera photo + question) still uses the model.

Make the model load on fallback (the shipped position)

  1. In python/pocketinfer/applications/nomad_right/constants.py set QUERY_FALLBACK = "qwen" and keep LLM_FALLBACK_ENABLED = True (both must be on).

  2. Keep LLM_BACKEND = "llama-server" so the model is loaded on demand; with "ollama" the Ollama service must be running.

  3. Restart the kiosk. Proof in the terminal on the first unanswered welfare question: a [QWEN] … LLM_FALLBACK or RAG-grounded generation line, and llama_server.log grows under /tmp/nomad_right_logs/.

Remove the model from questions completely

  1. In the same file set QUERY_FALLBACK = "sorry". Nothing else needs to change; LLM_FALLBACK_ENABLED is ignored in this position.

  2. Optionally reword the apology in QUERY_SORRY_TEXT right below it. It has an English, a Hindi and a Tamil version and is spoken as written, without the translation step, so keep all three in step.

  3. Restart the kiosk. Proof in the terminal: QUERY_FALLBACK=sorry - nothing found, apology spoken and APOLOGY ta (predefined …) on an unanswered question, and no llama-server process appears (pgrep -a llama-server stays empty).

Try it without editing: the environment variable wins for one run, so NOMADRIGHT_QUERY_FALLBACK=sorry pocketinfer-retro starts the kiosk in apology mode and the next plain pocketinfer-retro is back on the file's setting.

Measured on 13 September 2026 with the model stubbed: over twelve varied questions, only one (“my employer has not paid my wages”) reached the model in the "qwen" position; the scheme questions were answered identically in both positions, and the off-topic ones got the apology in both. Live in "sorry" mode the apology arrived 0.6 s after a typed question, in Tamil after selecting Tamil and in Hindi after selecting Hindi, with no model process.

Speech recognition · ~/bhashini_models/asr/asr_config.json

"backends": { "hi": "conformer", "ta": "whisper-ct2", "en": "english" },
"english":  { "backend": "whisper-ct2-en", "ct2_model_dir": ".../asr/english_models/whisper-base.en",
              "vosk_model_dir": ".../asr/english_models/vosk-model-small-en-us-0.15" }

English is loaded on the first English request, not at start, so it costs nothing until a citizen chooses English. Two engines are installed: whisper-ct2-en (Whisper base.en, CTranslate2 int8 on the GPU, about 0.3 s per utterance) and vosk (the 40 MB CPU model, about 1.6 s, more errors). Switch with "backend" and restart the service. A better English model, small.en, is kept under ~/asr_english_candidates/; point ct2_model_dir at it for fewer errors at roughly twice the latency and more memory (numbers in the rebuild report).

Tamil options: whisper-ct2 (in use: CTranslate2 int8, word error 12 % on FLEURS and 7 % on kiosk phrases, 2.6 s, 0.7 GB less memory) or whisper (TensorRT, 12 % / 5 %, 1.6 s, more memory). conformer for Tamil is broken (75 % errors) and is only there for Hindi. After editing: sudo systemctl restart bhashini_models.service.

6 · Forms, questions and words

Everything the kiosk says in the form flow is a string in nomadright/forms/build_forms.py; nothing is translated at run time. After any edit, rebuild the catalogue and restart the kiosk:

cd ~/Retro/NOMADRIGHT/pocket-infer-sw/nomadright/forms
python3 build_forms.py            # writes forms.json; prints "8 forms, 166 fields, 24 prompts"
PROMPTS
The 24 shared sentences (confirm the form, "you said X, correct?", "show your passbook", "sent", "saved, will be sent", the review sentences) in en, hi, ta. Keep the {placeholders}.
WORDS
What counts as yes, no, repeat, change, skip and cancel, per language. Add regional variants here; the recogniser's exact spelling matters, so take them from the kiosk log when a person's word was not understood.
Q
The question bank: one entry per field key with its three questions. Reworded questions go here.
CHOICES
Spoken synonyms for choice fields (gender, relation, yes/no, KYC document …).
FORMS
The eight forms, each with its title in three languages, the title patterns and keywords the OCR matcher uses, look-alike discriminators, and the field list in the form's printed order.

Adding a form

  1. Save the official PDF under nomadright/forms/sources/ and note its title as printed, in every script it comes in.

  2. Append a dict to FORMS copying the PMSBY entry: form_id, scheme_id (one of the kiosk's schemes), title and spoken_name in three languages, title_patterns (exact printed title words), keywords with weights (words that appear only on this form score 3.0), discriminators against any look-alike form, supported_languages, and fields.

  3. Build fields in the printed order with the helpers: field(key, type), doc_field(key, type, document, spoken_question) for values read from a document, choice(key, name) for fixed options. Types: name, text, address, integer, number, amount, phone, pincode, date, email, aadhaar, account_number, ifsc, choice, group. Add required=False for optional fields, condition={"field": …, "equals": …} or {"field": …, "minor": True} for fields that only apply sometimes, and a group with count_from and max_items for repeated rows such as household members. New field keys need a question in Q.

  4. Rebuild, then prove the matcher on synthetic photos before trusting it:

    cd ~/Retro/NOMADRIGHT/pocket-infer-sw/python
venv/bin/python pocketinfer/applications/nomad_right/benchmarks/make_form_photos.py
venv/bin/python pocketinfer/applications/nomad_right/benchmarks/form_id_benchmark.py --json /tmp/form_id.json

    Accept the change only with zero WRONG rows. Then photograph the printed form with the kiosk's own webcam and press Camera.

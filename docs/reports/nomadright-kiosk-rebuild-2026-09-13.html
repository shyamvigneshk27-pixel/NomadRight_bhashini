2 · The form controls: why they did not work, what was fixed

  1. The screen stayed on "Reading the form". After OCR the flow asked "Is this the X form?" or "which scheme is it for?" aloud but never changed the screen state, so the panel kept the IDENTIFYING state, in which Hold-and-speak, Repeat, Change and Skip are disabled. The person heard a question and could not answer it; after 90 s the session timed out. Fixed by pushing the CONFIRM_FORM state and the question text before every such listen.
  2. Cancel and the commands were dropped or misread during identification and while a document was awaited. Fixed: the cancel word ends the session anywhere; Repeat, Change and Skip are handled while a document is awaited.
  3. A press during the spoken question was lost. Fixed: a press on Hold-and-speak, or on any command, stops the speech immediately and is acted on; the playback thread is joined before the next prompt so prompts never overlap.
  4. A playback hiccup cancelled the session. A failed or stopped playback used to be treated like Home. Fixed: only Home ends the session; a playback failure is logged and the text stays on screen.
  5. Touch reliability. The hold button now captures the pointer, so a finger drifting off the button keeps recording until it lifts; long-press context menus are suppressed.
  6. Change. Reopens only the answer just given (or the field just skipped), never further back, as you decided. Skip keeps the existing three re-asks on required fields, then unanswered_required.

3 · Screens, header, assets

Home is the language page. Every Home button calls the backend's Home (which cancels a form in progress and wipes its answers, leaving sealed forms in the outbox untouched) and returns there. The language is set once, there, and drives the screen strings, the recogniser, the spoken answers and the form questions; no other screen offers a language control. The menu page has Speak Your Question, Scan a Document, View All Schemes and Help (five numbered steps in the chosen language). The question page shows the answer at 30 px with Listen again, which replays the last spoken answer from memory. The form page shows the current question at 34 px with a compact hold button and three commands. After sending, the thank-you page stays until Home.

Header. Emblem, "भारत सरकार / GOVERNMENT OF INDIA", the live date and time from the Jetson's clock (updated every second, never from the network) and the Home button. No settings, microphone, brightness or language controls. A one-line notice appears under the header only while the on-device backend is unreachable; it names no address, port or certificate. The receiver side is covered by the form flow's own "saved, will be sent automatically" message.

Admin. The settings sidebar is gone from the user-facing screens. A tiny "Admin" text in the corner of the language page opens the PIN prompt (9999, verified on the device) and then the panel with audio, display, status, diagnostics, logs and the terminal, unchanged.

The emblem. You supplied a generated image of the State Emblem with the lockup and asked for it to be used; it is bundled as emblem.png, unmodified apart from cropping and scaling. Two things to know before this kiosk goes in front of the public: your own brief ruled out an AI-generated emblem in favour of an official asset, and the State Emblem of India (Prohibition of Improper Use) Act 2005 restricts the emblem and any imitation of it to authorised use. If the pilot has that authorisation, the official asset from india.gov.in can replace this file in one step; if it does not, the header works without the image.

Artwork. The loading screen and the thank-you screen use the NomadRight illustration you supplied, compressed to 115 KB. The other pages keep a faint monument-and-crane skyline drawn in code. No image-generation tool exists in this environment, so nothing else was generated; nothing is fetched at run time.

7 · The clock without internet

The header uses the Jetson's system clock, timezone Asia/Kolkata. Today the clock was NTP-synchronised. The board has two real-time clocks; rtc0 (the power-sequencer RTC) held the correct time and is the one the system reads at boot, rtc1 reads 1970 and is unused. Whether rtc0 survives a power cut depends on the coin cell on the carrier board's RTC header, which the developer kit ships without. Without it, an offline cold boot resumes from the time of the last shutdown and the header shows a stale date.

Power-cut test, to run once: disconnect Wi-Fi, note the time on the kiosk header, pull the power, wait ten minutes, power on still offline, and compare the header time with a watch. Right means the RTC is battery-backed; ten minutes behind means it is not, and a CR2032 on the RTC header fixes it. Nothing in the software will change either way.

9 · Voice interaction fixes (evening of 13 September)

Your second brief: a clean session per person, Listen again tied to the completed answer, tap-to-speak on forms, greetings and questions about the kiosk, and tolerance for recognition errors in scheme names. Everything below was tested on the live kiosk over its own socket; the log lines quoted are the kiosk's.

What was wrong

  • Stale answer after Home. Home reset the scheme-intelligence session but never the on-screen answer object, the English text or the stored answer audio, so the next person could replay the previous person's answer. Worse, the question screen derived its answer text from the screen's bottom line, so after Home it showed the Home hint "Hold: Voice Q&A" as if it were an answer (your first photo).
  • Listen again was a permanent button whenever any text was on screen, with no notion of a completed answer.
  • Greetings and questions about the device ("नमस्ते", "what can you do", "who are you") translated well and then fell to the apology; the intent engine only knows scheme intents.
  • Recognition errors in scheme names. The existing phonetic corrector (scheme_intel/repository.py, _phonetic_key + correct()) works on the English text after translation. Your example "इनडिएस योजना के लिए महनदंड क्या है" had already become "What is the penalty for the IndS scheme?" by then, beyond its reach, while the correctly spoken sentence is answered about PDS.

What changed

WhereChange
app.pyreplay_available is explicit runtime state published to the screen: true only after an answer's audio has played to the end, false while the next question is being answered, false on Home and language change. Home and a language change clear the answer object, the English text, the stored audio and the flag (_clear_answer_state). The current person's context is never touched between questions: the previous answer stays until a new one replaces it, a question that yields nothing leaves the last completed answer replayable, and the scheme-intelligence idle expiry is lifted (SESSION_TTL_S 5 min → 12 h safety net). The answer playback and replay stop at once when the speech button is pressed and listening starts (_play_interruptible, shared with the form prompts).
